# Affiliate integrity
- Never scrape marketplaces without written authorization.
- Never present demo pricing as live or rank based on commission.
- Separate products, variants, merchant offers, price observations and referrals.
- Redirect only to server-side approved and allowlisted destinations following deliberate clicks.
- Disclose affiliations; never drop cookies, manipulate attribution or fabricate trends.
